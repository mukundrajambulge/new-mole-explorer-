## Non-D3 supersession

The Final Acceptance Specification's Gate D3 labels for AT-0141, AT-0146 and AT-0204 conflict with the current Roadmap/PHD-V2-15 D5/D6 sequencing. Their table statuses follow the accepted Roadmap sequencing, but no approved amendment reconciling the labels was found. Do not describe all 23 tests as completed or the authority mismatch as formally resolved. This traceability conflict does not change the independent mandatory AT-0058 failure.

Historical `AT-0103` language requiring 70 physical/raw channels is superseded by its v1.1/v1.2 amendments: 80 logical IDs and the conditional 59-array physical map are operative. AT-0103 remains Level 6 / Gate D6 for representation, serialization/cache compatibility and resources; it is not a D3 direct-versus-grid threshold.

The final matrix contains no `OPEN`, `TODO`, `UNKNOWN`, or `PENDING` statuses. AT-0058 is explicitly failed and prevents acceptance.
