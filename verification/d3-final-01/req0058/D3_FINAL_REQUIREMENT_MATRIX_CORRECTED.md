## Gate assignment authority

The Final Acceptance Specification's earlier Gate D3 labels for AT-0141, AT-0146 and AT-0204 are preserved as source history. The project owner's explicit instruction in this continuation approved a sequence reconciliation, recorded as a dated Roadmap/Source-of-Truth amendment: AT-0141 and AT-0146 follow the Roadmap/PHD-V2-15 D5 result sequence; AT-0204's compound requirement follows D5 for Q-score/discrete result semantics and D6 for numerical equivalence. These are each classified exactly `DEFERRED_BEYOND_D3_BY_AUTHORITATIVE_ROADMAP`, are not completed, and are not silently converted to PASS. The amendment explicitly supersedes the old Gate D3 labels for execution sequencing and this D3 disposition without changing their scientific acceptance criteria.

The other mandatory D3 requirements remain as in the preserved historical matrix. The earlier HOLD decision remains unchanged as historical provenance. Current corrected disposition is in `D3_FINAL_DECISION_CORRECTED.md`.
