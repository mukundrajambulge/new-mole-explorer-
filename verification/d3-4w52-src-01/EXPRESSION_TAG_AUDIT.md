# Expression-tag audit

## Deposited facts

The deposited polymer has 172 positions. Positions 165–172 are LEHHHHHH and the current wwPDB validation report labels all eight as expression-tag residues. RCSB entity metadata identifies the source as T4 lysozyme, the host as E. coli BL21(DE3), and the plasmid name as pET29. Coordinates are modeled only through residue 164; positions 165–172 have no atom coordinates. The accessible primary article names T4 lysozyme/L99A and explicitly points to the SI for cloning, purification and crystallization details.

## Unresolved physical facts

The accessible 4W52 paper text and the SI correction notice do not establish whether the C-terminal tag was removed before crystallization, whether a protease site was present and used, or whether the tag remained in the sample but was disordered. The deposited sequence and validation classification are not proof that the tag was physically retained at crystallization. The absence of coordinates is not proof that the tag was cleaved. The vector label pET29 alone does not establish the exact insert junction or cleavage operation.

The corrected Supporting Information was not retrieved. An official PNAS supplement request returned 403 and the tested PMC supplement path returned 404. No exact construct map, purification notebook, cleavage result or author correspondence was found in the retained primary evidence.

## Disposition

Tag sequence in the deposited entity: LEHHHHHH. Tag annotation: expression tag. Coordinate status: unmodeled. Physical presence/cleavage before crystallization: unresolved. Protease site and cleavage: not established. No tag was reconstructed and none is assumed removed or retained for a prepared receptor.
