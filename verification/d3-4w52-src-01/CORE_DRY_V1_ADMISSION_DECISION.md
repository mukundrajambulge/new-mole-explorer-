# CORE_DRY_V1 admission decision

**Decision: NOT ADMITTED.** 4W52/BNZ is retired from the current CORE_DRY_V1 fixture path pending new primary evidence or author clarification.

The source passes the straightforward BNZ identity, local ligand completeness, water, EPE, metal and general quality checks. Its deposited sequence and coordinate state can be described exactly. However, the accessible 4W52 primary article delegates construct and purification details to a corrected SI that could not be retrieved. Available evidence does not establish whether LEHHHHHH was physically cleaved before crystallization or remained as an unmodeled extension, so it cannot establish whether Leu164 is the physical molecular terminus. R12G and I137R are deposited and modeled but their origin is unresolved. A preparation authorization would otherwise have to guess the physical construct or terminal chemistry.

This is a source admission decision only. It does not reject the PDB structure quality, assert that the benzene pose is invalid, or accept/reject the broader D3 scientific program. If authoritative evidence resolves the physical construct, a new gate may reconsider the fixture without repeating this source audit. No D3_PREP_DEC_03_PROMPT.md is created.
