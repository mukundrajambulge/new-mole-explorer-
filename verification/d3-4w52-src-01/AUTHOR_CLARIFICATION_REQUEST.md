# Author clarification request — 4W52 construct and C-terminal state

Not sent. Prepared for a later owner-approved contact.

Subject: 4W52 T4 lysozyme construct and C-terminal tag state

Dear authors,

I am auditing the source state of PDB entry 4W52 for a reproducible computational fixture. The deposited 4W52 entity sequence is 172 residues, with G12/A99/R137 and a C-terminal LEHHHHHH extension; coordinates are modeled through L164. The article's Methods refer to the SI Appendix for cloning and purification, and the published correction notice says the SI was corrected online. I have not been able to retrieve the corrected SI file.

Could you please clarify the following for the protein sample used to crystallize 4W52?

1. Was the C-terminal LEHHHHHH tag enzymatically cleaved before crystallization, or was it physically present in the crystal but disordered and unmodeled? If it was cleaved, what was the cleavage site/protease and exact final residue/sequence?
2. Was the expressed and crystallized construct sequence exactly the deposited sequence, including G12 and R137 as well as L99A? If known, were R12G and I137R intentional substitutions, inherited background variants, or differences introduced by a particular clone?
3. The deposited BNZ occupancy is 0.70, while the single modeled F-helix backbone across residues 108–114 has occupancy 0.90. The article's Fig. 2 caption says ligand poses were assigned to protein conformations by matching occupancy. Could you clarify the intended 4W52 state assignment and whether a second F-helix state was observed but not modeled?
4. If available, could you point me to the corrected SI Methods or exact plasmid/construct record that documents the insert and purification/cleavage steps?

The distinction matters because, if the tag remained but was disordered, the modeled L164 is a coordinate endpoint rather than the physical protein terminus. I will not infer a terminal state from the deposited sequence or missing coordinates alone.

Thank you for any clarification you can provide.
