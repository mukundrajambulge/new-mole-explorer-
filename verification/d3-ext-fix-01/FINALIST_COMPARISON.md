## Independent validation evidence

Official full wwPDB validation PDFs for all four candidates are retained under source_artifacts/validation/ and included in SHA256SUMS.txt. For 4W52 BNZ, the report gives RSCC 0.93, RSR 0.10, B-factor min/median/95th-percentile/max 12/12/14/15 Å², no ligand bond or angle outliers, and sub-0.9 occupancy for all six atoms. Protein density outliers are at terminal positions 162–164, including Leu164 RSRZ 10.0. For 5JWT BNZ, the report gives RSCC 0.96, RSR 0.06, B 12/12/13/15 Å² and no ligand geometry outliers; protein density outliers include positions 109–114 in the ligand shell. The selected 4EMN BEN instance A401 has RSCC 0.97, RSR 0.05, B 10/12/19/21 Å², no bond-length outlier and three assessed bond-angle outliers. 7FEZ 4I1 has RSCC 0.99, RSR 0.05, B 4/7/9/9 Å², no bond/angle outlier and two ligand torsion outliers. Validation PDFs provide structure-quality context only and do not settle state, construct or component policy.

4W52 is the strongest lead because the ligand graph is simple, its audited pocket has no water within 5 Å and no metal, and a conservative field-size estimate is below the current resource cap. Its ligand has good density fit and no bond or angle outliers, but all six atoms have sub-unity occupancy; the protein validation report also flags residues 162–164 at the modeled endpoint. It still fails the hard source/construct gate: whether the physical sample retained or cleaved the expression tag and the provenance of the two deposited variants remain unresolved, and the modeled endpoint cannot safely be assigned terminal chemistry until primary construction records are obtained. Under the selection rule, a promising lead is not an admissible fixture.

5JWT does not improve the T4L path because it has missing terminal residues, alternate local receptor conformations and protein density outliers near the ligand shell. 4EMN has good BEN density fit but three angle outliers in the selected instance, an unresolved BEN chemical state, a near-site sulfate and six missing Arg atoms. 7FEZ has excellent ligand density fit and complete polymer sequence but has two ligand torsion outliers and uses a flexible long-chain acid with uncertain carboxyl state and four nearby receptor alternate residues. None satisfies all hard requirements.
