## Selection rule outcome

Only 3DMX answers **YES** to the requested preparation-feasibility question. Its source-record identity is explicit: the deposited engineered protein is the T4 lysozyme L99A hydrophobic-cavity construct with the additional listed C54T and C97A substitutions, expressed in *E. coli*, and the selected deposited receptor/ligand coordinates are the complete chain-A/BNZ complex. This is not a claim that the mutant is wild type or that preparation is authorized. It is sufficient source resolution to move the already-selected pair directly to the DEC-04 preparation-decision gate.

The 3DMX primary study describes benzene binding in an internal hydrophobic cavity. The crystallographic solvent condition (pH 6.9, 277 K, concentrated phosphate) is evidence about crystal growth and is not assigned as the future preparation pH. The future pH, receptor protonation, hydrogen policy, altloc A rule, and remote-component policy remain explicit DEC-04 decisions.

No unresolved structure, residue, ligand heavy atom, or direct essential-water path survives in the selected pair. There is no metal/cofactor within 8 Å. The farthest receptor alternate atoms are outside 5 Å and have near-identical minimum ligand distances between A and B; their deterministic major-state choice is a recorded preparation policy question, not a pocket-geometry ambiguity.
