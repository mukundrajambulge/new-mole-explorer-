## Exact blockers

1. **Site-relevant receptor heavy atoms are missing.** The source audit records 21 unobserved heavy-atom rows across seven observed residues. Four incomplete sidechains flank the RTL pocket: precursor/label 101 ASP (author 85) lacks CB, CG, OD1, OD2; 103 LEU (author 87) lacks CG, CD1, CD2; 104 ASN (author 88) lacks CG, OD1, ND2; and 105 GLU (author 89) lacks CB, CG, CD, OE1, OE2. The nearest *observed atom in each residue* is 6.3939, 3.6203, 3.9869, and 6.5587 Å from RTL, respectively; these are not distances to the absent atoms. Actual missing-atom positions are unknown. These residues cannot be certified remote from the pocket. PHD-V2-03 REC-D06 says missing binding-site heavy atoms block ordinary preparation without a separately validated repair profile and explicit authorization. No such repair profile is approved, and speculative heavy-atom reconstruction is out of scope.
2. **Unmodeled physical N-terminal residue / state representation.** Mature Leu17 is part of the natural protein after the cleaved precursor signal peptide, but has no coordinates; modeled Ile18 is therefore not the physical mature N terminus. The PHD-V2-03 terminal rule requires polymer connectivity and source evidence to determine termini, and prohibits equating a coordinate break with a biological terminus. The D2 PreparedReceptorStateV1 type stores explicit graph, chemistry, coordinates, assembly, model, chain and altloc selections, but has no structured missing-polymer/terminal-continuation field. Its coordinate validator requires coordinates for every graph atom. No approved profile demonstrates how to preserve Ile18 as an internal peptide residue while omitting Leu17 without a false terminal cap, invalid topology, or speculative heavy-atom reconstruction. Reconstruction is prohibited for this gate.
3. **AEPE loop remoteness is only an inference.** Precursor residues 127–130 (mature 111–114, AEPE) are absent. The predecessor compared homolog 1GX8 and found corresponding residues about 11.17–14.65 Å from its RTL coordinates (nearest reported residue: Pro113, 11.1668 Å). Those are homolog coordinates, not 9I7O observations. PHD-V2-03 requires site relevance to be evaluated and sets no universal distance threshold. A structural biologist has not approved preserving this gap as remote. No loop reconstruction is authorized.
4. **Receptor chemical state is not explicit.** The source-condition pH can be interpreted (see below), but it does not determine residue microstates. There is no owner-approved pH/context, residue-level protonation and histidine assignment, terminal assignment, hydrogen placement/orientation, hydrogen optimization, or side-chain orientation policy for this receptor. PHD-V2-03/15 require an explicit resolved state and place automatic pKa/protonation generation outside ordinary V1.
5. **No exact approved preparation profile/toolchain exists.** The canonical SOT says no preparation profile is approved. Meeko v0.8.0 is reference evidence only, not an approved production preparer. Automatic pKa/protonation, tautomer generation, conformer/minimization, and rotor/root generation are experimental or unvalidated. No software package/executable hash, dependency lock, environment/container digest, exact command/options, allowed transformations, warning/error policy, or output validation profile has been approved. D2 state sealing validates already-resolved explicit objects; it is not a chemical preparation toolchain.
6. **Required owner and human approvals are absent.** No owner decision authorizes the selected natural major-A coordinate state, preparation pH/context, receptor microstates, terminal/gap handling, missing-sidechain handling, or exact toolchain. No independent human structural-biology or computational-chemistry review has been performed for this authorization gate. The prior cohort-level “qualified yes” was an internal AI source review for progression to this prompt, not human expert approval or preparation authorization.
7. **Ligand kinematic/scorer torsion quantities are not sealed.** CCD graph inspection supports one candidate ordinary search rotor, C14–C15, because it moves the terminal CH2OH heavy-atom group; the ring bonds and conjugated polyene bonds are not search rotors, and terminal H-only rotations are excluded. This is Codex analysis, not a sealed LigandKinematicModel. No root/tree/profile or exact model digest exists. The PHD-V2-06 Vina scorer quantity N_tors_vina is distinct and cannot be replaced by this candidate search_torsion_count; no approved execution representation or scorer-quantity derivation was made.
8. **No prepared states or SearchRegion are sealed.** A source-coordinate envelope is recorded in SOURCE_AND_STATE_FREEZE.md, but no PreparedReceptorState, PreparedLigandState, or SearchRegion object/profile/digest is defined or generated. The future full-pose experiment is development-only; this gate does not freeze an experiment box by inference.

Each of blockers 1, 2, 4, 5, and 6 is independently sufficient to stop execution. Blockers 3 and 7 also require explicit expert/profile resolution before exact state sealing.
