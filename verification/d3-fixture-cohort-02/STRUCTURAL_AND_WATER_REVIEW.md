## Candidate `9I7O`

- **Deposited state:** one X-ray model at 2.00 Å. Protein entity 1 is chain A. `RTL` is entity 2, asym ID B / author chain A, residue 500, all 21 heavy atoms present, occupancy 0.720. It is noncovalently bound. Retinol's CCD graph types as 19 `C_H`, one `C_P`, and one `O_DA`; CORE_DRY_V1 covers all three.
- **Assembly:** one deposited protein instance; RCSB's author-assigned biological assembly 1 is monomeric. Use chain A/deposited asymmetric unit explicitly as the receptor state. No unrequested oligomer reconstruction is needed for the source state.
- **Conformation:** the record contains natural A/B polymorphs at mature positions 64 and 118 (precursor 80 and 134), plus Phe105 rotamers. The major A coordinates have occupancy about 0.746 at the two polymorphic sites and 0.720 for the major Phe105 rotamer; retinol occupancy is 0.720. The minor B Phe105 rotamer (0.280) overlaps RTL (minimum heavy-atom distance 1.494 Å); the major A rotamer avoids that clash (minimum heavy-atom separation 3.345 Å). Select and record the coherent major A coordinates if preparation is later authorized. This is a natural variant mixture: it is not an engineered mutant and the crystal occupancy is not a measurement of the milk lot's genotype proportions.
- **Sequence/termini:** the natural bovine precursor is 178 residues. Its signal peptide is residues 1–16; mature protein begins at Leu17. Deposited coordinates start at precursor 18, so mature Leu17 is unmodeled. Do not rebuild it. Internal positions 127–130 (mature 111–114, sequence AEPE) are also unresolved. They are not observed in this model; a homolog comparison places the corresponding segment away from RTL, which is an inference rather than a 9I7O coordinate observation. Keep the gap and flag it for authorization; do not rebuild it.
- **Other components:** the deposited complex has 35 waters and no metals, cofactors, or other non-water non-polymers. Nearest modeled water is 4.036 Å from RTL C18; nearest to retinol O1 is about 6.57 Å. No deposited water bridges the retinol hydroxyl. The primary article describes retinol accommodated in the hydrophobic beta-lactoglobulin calyx. Geometry alone cannot prove water is nonessential; however, there is no direct crystallographic water bridge at O1 and no identified literature requirement for water-supported retinol recognition. Water omission is therefore compatible with the current dry-profile source review, with the limitation disclosed.
- **Quality:** 2.00 Å; R-free about 0.197; overall clashscore 4.71; no Ramachandran outliers. Full-ensemble ligand/side-chain clashes arise from the minor Phe105 alternate state. The official validation report notes minor retinol bond/angle deviations; there are no retinol torsion, ring, or chirality outliers. These remain visible as evidence, not silently normalized.
- **Crystallization pH:** repository metadata records pH 7.4; the paper's detailed methods state protein buffer and reservoir at pH 8.5. For the crystallization-condition source, the explicit experimental method supports pH 8.5. Preserve the repository discrepancy in the authorization prompt and require the later authorizing reviewer to affirm the pH-dependent preparation condition.
